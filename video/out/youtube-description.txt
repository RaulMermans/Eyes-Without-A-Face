Eyes Without a Face — a privacy-first hybrid AI integration builder for #BuildHybridAI 2026.

Private CRM records stay in the browser. Gemini Nano can read field names on-device. Gemini in the cloud sees only a Phantom Twin — aliases and invented examples — after you approve one exact request. The mapping runs locally. Structure and meaning are reported separately. The export is a Node package with no records inside.

This video walks the current three-stage product on fictional data. The on-screen mapping in the product segment is the labelled simulated test planner, not a live Gemini call, so every screen is visible without waiting on the provider. Architecture, checks, and privacy rules are the real product.
